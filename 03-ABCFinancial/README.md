# ABC Financial — Balanced Scorecard

> **Category:** 💰 Finance & Strategic Performance

## Overview

Executive balanced scorecard for ABC Financial aligning financial, customer, internal process, and learning & growth perspectives.

## Business Questions

- Are we meeting strategic KPI targets?
- Which perspective is underperforming?
- How do leading indicators relate to financial outcomes?

## Key Metrics

| Perspective | Examples |
|-------------|----------|
| Financial | Revenue growth, ROI, cost ratio |
| Customer | NPS, retention, share of wallet |
| Internal | Cycle time, error rate, utilization |
| Learning & Growth | Training hours, employee satisfaction |

## Data Sources

- GL / financial statements
- CRM customer metrics
- HR & operations feeds

## Report File

Open **`ABC_Balanced_Scorecard.pbix`** in Power BI Desktop.

## Features

- Traffic-light KPI status vs. target
- Trend sparklines by perspective
- Drill-through to detail pages
- Period comparison (YTD, QoQ)
